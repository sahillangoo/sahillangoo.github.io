In modern software development, it is dangerously easy to confuse complexity with sophistication. Engineering teams often adopt heavyweight microservice topologies, multi-layered state managers, and hyper-abstracted frameworks long before their domain demands it.

## The Power of Restraint

Restraint is an active discipline. It means choosing boring, battle-tested tools when they solve the problem completely. It means writing straightforward functions instead of sprawling inheritance trees.

### Key Tenets

- **YAGNI (You Aren't Gonna Need It)**: Implement things only when you actually need them, never when you just foresee that you need them.
- **KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid)**: Most systems work best if they are kept simple rather than made complicated.
- **DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself)**: Eliminate duplicate logic, but recognize when code is coincidentally similar rather than genuinely coupled.

Simplicity is not a compromise; it is the highest mark of engineering mastery.
