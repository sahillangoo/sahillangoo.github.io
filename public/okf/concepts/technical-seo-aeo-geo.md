# Technical SEO, GEO & Agentic Search Engineering

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) expand classical search optimization to ensure content is accurately parsed, cited, and recommended by LLM search agents.

## Core Mechanisms

1. **Princeton GEO Principles**: Structuring content with empirical citations (+40% visibility), concrete statistics (+37%), and authoritative expert quotations (+30%).
2. **Extractable Passage Architecture**: Leading content sections with concise 40 to 60 word definition and answer blocks directly under H2/H3 question headers.
3. **Structured Data Knowledge Graphs**: Embedding connected Schema.org JSON-LD graphs (Person, Organization, TechArticle, ProfessionalService, FAQPage) linking to authoritative knowledge graph entities.
4. **Agent Protocol Endpoints**: Serving machine-readable endpoints (`/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/pricing.md`, `/okf/`) for autonomous buyer and research agents.
